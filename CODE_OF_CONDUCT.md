# Code of conduct

Be kind and be useful. People here are learning to mod a game; some have never written code.

- Treat everyone with respect. No harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Assume good faith, explain your reasoning, and accept that "I don't know" is a fine answer.
- Keep discussion about the project. No spam, no malware, no sharing of pirated game files.
- Credit other people's work, including other mod loaders and their authors.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and block accounts that break these rules. To report a problem, use a private security advisory or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.
